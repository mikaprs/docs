## How to build a form in minutes

### 1. Sign up or log in

Sign up [here](https://app.fillout.com/signup) or log in [here](https://app.fillout.com/login) using any email or your Google account.

### 2. Choose a form type

On your **home** page, click `+ Make a Fillout` , then choose between a **Blank form**, **Template**, or **Connect** with 3rd party app integrations like Airtable, Notion, Hubspot, and more.

You can always connect to an [integration](https://www.notion.so/Integrations-ff23eebebba04d728aecd4744d814b81?pvs=21) later.

### 3. Customize the design

Click `Theme` to pick from one of the pre-made themes or [customize your own](https://www.notion.so/Themes-f29afca7098d44eda5f076c16b490868?pvs=21).

<img height="723" width="1343" src="https://file.notion.so/f/f/2a7ed980-5add-4b51-82ab-889415f57134/2642a77e-e05b-4565-8208-fc0c6b0ec428/Screenshot_2025-01-15_at_4.10.25_PM.png?table=block&id=17c0ee47-667b-8094-a19a-d559959584e3&spaceId=2a7ed980-5add-4b51-82ab-889415f57134&expirationTimestamp=1741788000000&signature=Aq4rGnymq8cXA45GYXHmuZP2Y2jO91ykQNzFJbg_OZQ&downloadName=Screenshot+2025-01-15+at+4.10.25%E2%80%AFPM.png" />

### 4. Add questions or fields

Click or drag and drop your desired [field types](https://www.notion.so/Field-types-1aa7ec8c6ad941c782be2af823ab3459?pvs=21) from the left-hand panel. Choose between over 40 different types.

<img height="723" width="1343" src="https://file.notion.so/f/f/2a7ed980-5add-4b51-82ab-889415f57134/39fec14a-0aa3-4764-94ed-873fefc76c7a/Screenshot_2025-01-15_at_4.12.23_PM.png?table=block&id=17c0ee47-667b-800f-8c1a-ff5d613e4a4f&spaceId=2a7ed980-5add-4b51-82ab-889415f57134&expirationTimestamp=1741788000000&signature=5GFSHHTCoNQVkQM0AR-O7QxJB6Hpx5q27yzOWjBzIRo&downloadName=Screenshot+2025-01-15+at+4.12.23%E2%80%AFPM.png" />

Then, enter the text and edit it in-line. Click its `settings icon` to customize it in the right-hand panel.

### 5. Publish your form

`Preview` your form to test it. Once everything's good to go, press `Publish`.

<img height="722" width="1342" src="https://file.notion.so/f/f/2a7ed980-5add-4b51-82ab-889415f57134/2833a88a-3c59-4e47-bb4e-aeac5fb0082d/Screenshot_2025-01-15_at_4.10.41_PM.png?table=block&id=17c0ee47-667b-80f5-84ac-e2f460e4d72e&spaceId=2a7ed980-5add-4b51-82ab-889415f57134&expirationTimestamp=1741788000000&signature=DF-m-ze9FNXa4md-ZlLWDWcM-VFu62qb6p7IrFc70Sw&downloadName=Screenshot+2025-01-15+at+4.10.41%E2%80%AFPM.png" />

That’s it! Share your **form link** anywhere or **embed your form** on any website. To view form submissions, head over to [**Results**](https://www.notion.so/Results-e59927a7827a4b01baf91a2d8d0afb59?pvs=21) in the top menu.
